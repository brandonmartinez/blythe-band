Ludington, Michigan · 2005–2007

blythe was a Christian rock band of five high-school kids who played battles of the bands, church basements, and a harvest festival in Walkerville. This is their website, rebuilt from the original database twenty years later.

the e.p.

Four songs mixed down on June 26, 2007 — two new (the beginning, when we fall) and two re-recorded from the black e.p. with ollie on vocals. This is the last work the band finished.

It never made it onto the website. By mid-2007 the audio player was returning server errors, and the site came down before these were posted. They sat as WAV masters on a hard drive for nineteen years. This is the first time they have been online.

joshua dumas — solo recording, 2007

Two weeks after dumas joined, the band’s March 10, 2006 news post said he “even showed us one of his own” and put a preview of it in the audio section. This is his song — written and recorded by him alone, not by blythe. The file that survived is a fuller 2007 recording, and it spent the last twenty years misfiled in another band’s folder. video

Five full sets, an hour and a half of footage, shot on a camcorder between February 2005 and April 2006. They survived because the band’s bassist burned them to two DVDs at the time and Erik still had them thirteen years later. They were uploaded to YouTube in October 2018 and are embedded here.

The first three predate the website entirely — blythe did not have one in 2005, and the shows list below only starts in 2006. This footage is the only record of them.

Each video’s description carries its own recording date, and three of those dates are confirmed by other people’s records — the college’s press release and events page, and the Ludington visitors bureau calendar. Those documents are reproduced here in full, so they survive whatever happens to the sites that hosted them.

war of the rockers — the tiki

The longest set of the five, and the last show on this page. The site announced it for the Stearn’s Motor Inn ball room and the tape calls it the Tiki — the Tiki was the room inside Stearn’s. This is the one show the local paper covered. The Ludington Daily News ran a photo from it the following Monday, which fixes the date, the room, and something the band’s own site never recorded: the night was a benefit.

“Debra Bethell-Romer sings ‘Stay with me’ during Friday night’s Battle of the Rockers at the Tiki. Tiki owner Randy Bowden donated the use of the club for the Battle of the Rockers to raise money for Ludington’s new skate plaza, to be built north of the Jaycee Mini Golf at Stearns Park.” — Ludington Daily News, front page photo caption, Apr 24, 2006. Photo by Andy Klevorn.

the story of this page

blythe put a website up in February 2006 and bought blythe-band.com that same month. It ran on Ruby on Rails 1.1 with a Flash audio player — a genuinely ambitious thing for a high-schooler to build in 2006, and the band’s bassist built it.

By mid-2007 the audio player had started returning server errors, and the site was replaced by a single image pointing at the band’s PureVolume page. On February 9, 2008 the domain registration was cancelled. It expired that month, and blythe was gone from the web for eighteen years.

Everything above — every news post, show, biography, thank you, and song — was recovered in 2026 from the site’s original MySQL dump and its deployed files, both of which had survived on a hard drive. Nothing here was rewritten. The lowercase is theirs.

the e.p. is the exception, and the reason this page is more than a restoration. Those four mixdowns are dated June 26, 2007 — the same summer the website’s audio section broke. They were never posted, never pressed, and never heard outside the band. The WAV masters were sitting in a Logic archive the whole time. They were converted for this page in 2026 and are otherwise untouched.
