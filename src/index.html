Ludington, Michigan · 2005–2007

blythe was a Christian rock band of five high-school kids who played battles of the bands, church basements, and a harvest festival in Walkerville. This is their website, rebuilt from the original database twenty years later.

the e.p.

Four songs mixed down on June 26, 2007 — two new (the beginning, when we fall) and two re-recorded from the black e.p. with ollie on vocals. This is the last work the band finished.

It never made it onto the website. By mid-2007 the audio player was returning server errors, and the site came down before these were posted. They sat as WAV masters on a hard drive for nineteen years. This is the first time they have been online.

Two weeks after dumas joined, the band’s March 10, 2006 news post said he “even showed us one of his own” and put a preview of it in the audio section. Last Night's Show

Last night was amazing! We thought that our show would go down the tubes, with the schedule being all messed up and all; however, we were surprised but the number of loyal fans that we had! the story of this page

blythe put a website up in February 2006 and bought blythe-band.com that same month. It ran on Ruby on Rails 1.1 with a Flash audio player — a genuinely ambitious thing for a high-schooler to build in 2006, and the band’s bassist built it.

By mid-2007 the audio player had started returning server errors, and the site was replaced by a single image pointing at the band’s PureVolume page. On February 9, 2008 the domain registration was cancelled. It expired that month, and blythe was gone from the web for eighteen years.

Everything above — every news post, show, biography, thank you, and song — was recovered in 2026 from the site’s original MySQL dump and its deployed files, both of which had survived on a hard drive. Nothing here was rewritten. The lowercase is theirs.

the e.p. is the exception, and the reason this page is more than a restoration. Those four mixdowns are dated June 26, 2007 — the same summer the website’s audio section broke. They were never posted, never pressed, and never heard outside the band. The WAV masters were sitting in a Logic archive the whole time. They were converted for this page in 2026 and are otherwise untouched.
